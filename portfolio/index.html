1. Robot Model & Workstation Architecture

The demonstration features a custom 7-DOF Articulated Chess Manipulator Arm mounted alongside an 8x8 chess workstation (defined in urdf-files/chess_arm.urdf). The entire scene model consists of 18 links and 17 joints:

♟️ Chess Environment & Pieces
Features a sturdy base table (table_base), a mahogany-bordered chess board (chess_board), a regulation checkered playfield (board_surface), and chess pieces including the target white pawn at e2 (piece_pawn_e2), opposing black pawn at e7, white pawn at d2, white king at e1, black king at e8, and knight at g1.
🦾 5-Axis Articulated Arm
Kinematic chain featuring a base turntable yaw joint (arm_base_yaw, Z-axis), shoulder pitch joint (shoulder_pitch, Y-axis), elbow pitch joint (elbow_pitch, Y-axis), wrist pitch joint (wrist_pitch, Y-axis), and wrist roll joint (wrist_roll, X-axis) providing full 5-DOF spatial positioning and orientation across all 64 squares.
🤏 Parallel Jaw Gripper
A dual-jaw prismatic gripper (gripper_left_joint, gripper_right_joint) equipped with high-friction gold rubber finger pads calibrated to straddle and securely clamp a 24 mm diameter chess piece without collision.

Our custom URDF parser reads and validates every link origin, rotational axis, and joint limits. Visual elements support <box>, <cylinder>, <sphere>, and <material> color specifications parsed directly without external URDF packages.

2. Finite-State-Machine Choreography: 1. e4 Move

Autonomous piece manipulation is governed by the FiniteStateMachine runtime node running under make demo. The choreography is defined in fsm/chess_pick_and_place.json, decomposing the chess move into 9 sequential phases. Each state dispatches synchronized multi-joint setpoints to /joint_trajectory, servoed at 10 Hz by JointStatePublisher. Automatic transitions trigger upon reaching the target tolerance (ε = 0.03).

1 home yaw: 0.0, sh: -0.50, el: 1.10, grip: 0.022m Neutral standby stance above the board with jaws open wide. hover_e2

3. Custom Math, Kinematics & Viewer Architecture

In strict adherence to course specifications, no external kinematics, transform, or URDF-model libraries are used anywhere in the implementation (no KDL, Pinocchio, MoveIt, tf/tf2, or Eigen):

🧮 Custom 3D Rotation Math
Implemented entirely from first principles in include/math3d.hpp and src/math3d.cpp: 3D vector arithmetic, unit quaternions (axis-angle, fixed-axis roll-pitch-yaw, normalization, conjugation, Hamilton product), and 4x4 column-major transformation matrices (Matrix4::data[4*c + r]).
🌳 Recursive Tree Traversal
Implemented from scratch in src/forward_kinematics.cpp: traverses the validated robot tree from its discovered root link, composing local joint transformations T_origin · T_motion(q) across revolute, continuous, and prismatic joints to compute one root-relative pose per link.
🌐 World-State Composition
In src/robot_world_state_publisher.cpp, the runtime traverses the active TF tree and multiplies by the global root pose (T_global · T_parent · T_child), publishing complete 16-element column-major matrices on /xform_world at 10 Hz.

The external 3D viewer (scripts/viewer.py, web/) consumes /xform_world over raw TCP (no WebSocket gateways), mapping incoming column-major matrices to Three.js link objects with smooth 60 fps spherical linear quaternion interpolation (slerp), an interactive Animation Speed controller (0.2x–2.5x), and automated state dwell timing. Stale link objects are automatically purged whenever a model is reloaded.
